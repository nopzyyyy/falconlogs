Open this page on your phone then add it to your home screen as Falcon Logs. Follow the steps below to install it.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open this page on your iPhone in Safari.
  2. Sign in with your shop account on your phone if you are not already signed in.
  3. Tap the menu (three dots) in the bottom or top corner.
  4. Tap Share.
  5. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  6. Name it Falcon Logs and tap Add.
  7. Open the new Falcon Logs icon on your home screen. Do not use Safari after setup.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this page on your Android phone in Chrome.
  2. Sign in with your shop account on your phone if you are not already signed in.
  3. Tap the menu (three dots) in the top right.
  4. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  5. Confirm the app icon will appear as Falcon Logs.
  6. Open the Falcon Logs icon from your home screen. Do not browse to the app in Chrome.
